The ancient forest whispered secrets as Elara stepped beneath its emerald canopy. Sunlight filtered through the leaves, casting dappled shadows that danced across the moss-covered ground. She paused, her heart pounding, and drew a steadying breath.

This wasn't just a forest. It was a living, breathing testament to the magic that once flowed through these lands.

"You feel it too, don't you?" Kael's voice was barely above a whisper.

Elara nodded slowly. "It's like the trees remember."

"They do," he said, his eyes holding a mixture of wonder and sorrow. "Every root, every branch, every leaf—they carry the memories of a thousand years."

A shiver ran down her spine. Not from fear, but from something deeper, something she couldn't quite name.

They walked in comfortable silence, the only sound the soft crunch of leaves beneath their feet. Somewhere in the distance, an owl hooted.

"Kael," she said finally. "What if we're too late?"

He stopped and turned to face her, his expression softening. "Then we'll face it together. Whatever comes next."

She swallowed hard, a lump forming in her throat. For the first time in a long time, she felt hope.
